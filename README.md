# AuraCare — Blank-Slate Hair, Face & Body Studio (v2.3)

Starts completely blank with zero pre-filled personal answers or products, featuring **one-tap selectable option chips** across all 5 profile-building steps:
- **Step 1 (Basics & Environment):** Age range, skin tone/melanin context, city/GPS for live weather & AQI, shift/sleep schedule, indoor HVAC/hard water/pollution, and sensitivities.
- **Step 2 (Hair, Chemical History, Wigs/Braids & Rollers):** Selectable natural texture/thickness, **chemical/color history (Relaxed, Colored / Dyed, Bleached / Lightened, Natural / Virgin, Texturized / Permed, Keratin Treated, Frequent Heat)**, scalp/hair problem zones, wash frequency, hair goals, protective styles (wigs/braids), and curling/roller tools.
- **Step 3 (Face Skincare):** Selectable skin type, concerns, and routine complexity.
- **Step 4 (Body Care):** Selectable body skin concerns, cleansing formats (bar soap, body wash, scrub, shower oil), and moisture/finish preferences (**Body Lotion, Rich Cream, Whipped Body Butter, Body Oil, Homemade / DIY Body Powder, Dusting Powder / Talc-Free Powder, Lotion + Body Butter/Oil + Body Powder Layering**), plus an optional field for custom body powder or oil recipes.
- **Step 5 (Budget, Stores & Pro Care):** Selectable price cap, preferred stores, and Salon/Dermatologist/Esthetician interests.
- **Check-In Current Hair State:** Includes **Relaxed Hair (Out / Styled)** alongside **Natural / Unrelaxed Hair Out**, **Wearing a Wig (Unit On)**, **Wig Prep / Braids Underneath**, **Braid Extensions Installed**, and **Braid Takedown / Recovery**.

## Deploy to Vercel
1. Push this folder to a GitHub repository.
2. Import into **Vercel** and set `GROQ_API_KEY` in **Settings -> Environment Variables**.
3. Deploy and open in **Safari on iPhone -> Share -> Add to Home Screen**.
